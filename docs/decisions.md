# Product Decisions

This document records the foundational product and business decisions locked during the initial discovery (`/grill-me`) phase.

## Audience & Market

- **Target Audience:** Startups, SMEs, established businesses, and select larger clients that value premium design and strong technical implementation. The agency remains approachable, positioning itself as a high-quality partner rather than a massive enterprise consultancy.
- **Geographic Focus:** Indonesia-first, but fully available and positioned for international projects. The presentation will not feel limited to a local market.
- **Language:** English for V1. The site architecture will be localization-friendly for future Indonesian content. No language switcher is required for V1.
- **Tone:** Focused on clear business outcomes and technical credibility, presented with restrained editorial and design language. Avoids excessive developer jargon in primary marketing copy.

## Services

Top-level services are structured around client-buying intent:

1. **Website Design & Development**
2. **Web Applications**
3. **E-commerce**
4. **Website Redesign & Optimization**

_Supporting Capabilities:_ Frontend Development, Design Systems, Performance Optimization, Accessibility, SEO, and CMS Integration. These are detailed within the context of the top-level services.

## Conversion Strategy

- **Primary CTA:** "Discuss Your Project"
- **Secondary CTA:** "View Selected Work"
- **Contact Form Structure:** Name, Email, Company/Organization, Project Type, Budget Range (must include "Not sure yet"), Project Description. This operates as a frontend-only form with a clear integration boundary for V1.

## Portfolio Strategy

- **Structure:** Dedicated case study routes (`/work/[slug]`) rather than simple modals.
- **V1 Content:** Three clearly labeled fictional/demo case studies to demonstrate formatting without fabricating real client data or awards.
  1. Premium company/marketing website.
  2. E-commerce experience.
  3. Custom web application.

## Visual & Motion Identity

- **Visuals:** Light-dominant editorial composition with strategic dark sections for visual pacing. Near-white/cool-neutral surfaces and deep charcoal typography. Restrained cool-neutral/blue accent. High reliance on whitespace and typography, not decorative gradients or 3D/WebGL.
- **Glass:** Limited to elevated/functional surfaces.
- **Motion:** Low-to-medium intensity. CSS-first, reserved for structural reveals and tactile interaction feedback.
